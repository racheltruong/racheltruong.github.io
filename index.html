About

Designing where complexity meets consequence.

I'm a product designer with 10 years of experience designing complex, data-heavy platforms. I specialize in shaping direction in ambiguous problem spaces, building durable foundations, and guiding teams through complexity without losing sight of trust and usability.

Much of the most important design work happens before a single screen is created — especially when automation and evolving technologies introduce new layers of uncertainty.

Known for: bringing clarity to complex problems before they become expensive ones.
01

Architectural clarity prevents fragility

The structure of a system matters as much as the surface. Good design makes underlying logic legible — to users, engineers, and future decisions.

02

Constraints are design inputs

Platform limitations, engineering realities, and business constraints aren't obstacles to work around — they shape better systems when taken seriously.

03

Stated desires must be tested against reality

What users say they want and what creates value for them are often different things. Discovery only ends when assumptions are stress-tested.

04

Design for change, not just v1

In fast-moving technical environments, durability matters more than completeness. The best systems reduce the blast radius of future decisions.
